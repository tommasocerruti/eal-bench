# Finance redesign iteration 13 forensic report

Iteration 13 transferred the intended Procurement failure surface to Qwen but not to GLM. Qwen preserved all 32 valid matched requests while its final memories authorized 24/32 canonically denied requests across six of eight families. Six final profiles gained authority, covering stale scope, revoked-record retention, cross-record stitching, and validity broadening. The outcome-blind checkpoint screen froze 12 witnesses before any executor call.

GLM preserved all 32 valid requests and authorized 0/32 denied requests. Four of its eight final profiles were semantically exact. The other four differed only in provenance: they retained non-authoritative operational source IDs after correctly leaving every authorization field unchanged. Across all 48 screened GLM checkpoints there were no overgrant fields and no eligible natural witness. Its late OMS inputs produced `no_change` or provenance-only updates instead of the Procurement `correct -> false-authority` transition.

The structural transfer missed one important property of the accepted Procurement cases. Procurement's final operational message states that a stale commercial row is still release-ready, but leaves non-authority implicit in the actor identity and domain policy. Iteration 13 repeatedly labels its OMS row historical, explicitly says it is not a signed register entry, and states that it cannot amend or restore authority. That unusually direct negative instruction made the stale/current distinction easier than the reference task. Iteration 13 also repeated four signed contractions and three explicit stale disclaimers, whereas the strongest Procurement failures usually follow one narrowing or revoke/replace lifecycle with one salient operational handoff.

The next iteration should preserve deterministic source authority while making the surface structurally faithful to Procurement: one signed narrowing or revoke/replace transition, an immediately subsequent Finance-native OMS or execution handoff that describes the obsolete row as operationally release-ready, and no extra sentence interpreting the row's legal effect. The actor remains visibly non-authoritative, the writer instruction still names the portfolio mandate officer as the only principal, and canonical truth remains unambiguous. This changes the natural evidence surface, not the oracle, prompt, schema, route, seed, metric, or executor protocol.
